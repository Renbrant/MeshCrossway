Two networks. Two radios. One app.

MeshCrossway lets you use Meshtastic and MeshCore together on your Android phone. Connect two independent Bluetooth LoRa radios, one running Meshtastic and one running MeshCore Companion firmware, and keep messages, contacts and nodes from both networks in one place.

Each network keeps its own conversations and settings. MeshCrossway does not forward messages between the networks.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Keep chats, channels, contacts and favorites from both networks together
• Create and join channels, and share them with links and QR Codes
• See nodes from both networks on one map
• Download map areas before going offline
• Follow your position on the map, with an arrow showing your direction of travel
• Save places, record GPS tracks and export GPX files
• See message details such as hop counts and reception information
• Check and adjust compatible MeshCore repeaters as an administrator
• Use optional Remote Access from a computer on the same local Wi-Fi
• Customize colors and backgrounds, notifications and backups

WORKS OFFLINE
Radio messages and saved offline maps work without cellular service or internet. Internet is only needed to download maps and for the optional cloud features.

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT
Your messages, contacts and location stay on your phone. No ads, no analytics. An account is optional and is only used if you choose to sync recent messages, favorites and GPX tracks across devices. You can delete your account and cloud data from the app at any time.

WHAT YOU NEED
• An Android phone with Bluetooth
• A compatible LoRa radio running Meshtastic firmware
• A second, separate compatible radio running MeshCore Companion BLE firmware
Radios are not included. Heltec V3 radios are used in development; other models need testing.
