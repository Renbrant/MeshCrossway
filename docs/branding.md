# MeshCrossway — Official Brand Identity

> **APPROVED 2026-10-28.** This replaces all previous proposals and reconstructions of the MeshCrossway logo. The **single authoritative file** is [meshcrossway-logo.svg](site/assets/meshcrossway-logo.svg). Do not redraw, retype, recolor, restyle, or alter it without explicit approval.

## Approved official artwork

- **Master:** [Official MeshCrossway logo (SVG)](site/assets/meshcrossway-logo.svg)
- **Content:** the orange mountain-shaped symbol on the left, followed by the one-line `MeshCrossway` lettering, with `Mesh` in near-white and `Crossway` in orange.
- **No slogan in this lockup:** the approved production SVG contains neither `OFF-GRID COMMUNICATION` nor `EXPLORE · CONNECT · STAY IN TOUCH`.
- **Original proportions:** SVG `viewBox="0 0 330 44"`; scale uniformly only; transparent background.
- **Colors built into the approved SVG:** orange `#F07120` and near-white `#F2F2F2`. Do not remap the logo colors to site accent variables.
- **Lettering:** vector *paths traced from the supplied reference image*; not live text, not a licensed font file. Never reconstruct the letters using Eurostile, Microgramma, a system font, or a visually similar typeface.

The supplied reference was a 337 × 62 raster image containing the symbol, the main wordmark and two supporting lines. The **approved output for use** is the 330 × 44 SVG containing **only the symbol and wordmark**. It was created from that image, not from the original editable font or master vectors. Preserve the approved SVG file byte-for-byte when publishing or copying it.

## Usage rules

1. Use the exact master SVG for website navigation, project branding and other uses that display the official name with the symbol.
2. Maintain the original geometry, letter outlines, color fills, relative spacing and 330:44 aspect ratio.
3. Place it over a dark background for readable near-white lettering; do not add a background rectangle inside the file.
4. Do not use substitute text styled as the official logo; for ordinary body copy, existing website/UI typography is independent of the approved logo.
5. Approved 2026-11-09: the official symbol-only PNG and dark Android app icon are authorized derivatives. Use the exact approved symbol geometry and `#F07120`; do not redraw, recolor or restyle the official master. Other variants still require approval.
6. Do not add previously proposed gray/orange rings, alter the mountain peaks, or reintroduce the previous approximate SVGs.
7. Marketing illustrations and historical technical images are not logo masters. The logo's approval is independent of hardware validation, certification, or trademark clearance.

## Previous branding — retired

Previous file references such as `site/assets/logo-horizontal.svg`, `site/assets/logo-symbol.svg`, and `site/assets/logo-vertical.svg` were approximate concepts, **not** approved assets. They must not be used or linked as current branding. Earlier font suggestions and color guesses (including `#FF7A1A`) are **superseded** by the exact outlined master.

The original source font name and its commercial licensing terms have not been established. No claim of exclusive trademark rights or successful registration is made by this branding specification.
