## Official identity

- **Brand name:** **MeshCrossway** — capital M and C, always one word.
- **Symbol:** sharp, geometric **orange double mountain peaks**, with a small negative-space peak detail. The former gray/orange concentric-network rings are **retired** from the active identity.
- **Wordmark:** **Mesh** in near-white, **Crossway** in orange, on **one horizontal line**. Use a heavy, geometric, extended, technical sans-serif style.
- **Supporting line:** `OFF-GRID COMMUNICATION` in uppercase, widely tracked small text. Optional for small formats.
- **Positioning:** two LoRa networks, three independently powered devices, one interface. Meshtastic and MeshCore connect over BLE to the touchscreen Core.

## Color system

| Role | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Primary orange | `#FF7A1A` | mountain and `Crossway` |
| Near-white | `#F4F5F6` | `Mesh` on dark backgrounds |
| Dark navy/graphite | `#10151B` | logo presentation background |
| Cool-gray supporting text | `#AFB5BD` | subline and secondary information |

Use orange for positive emphasis, never blue as the brand accent. Network/status colors inside the UI may retain specific semantic meanings but must not redefine the official logotype.

## Typography

Display aesthetic: **geometric, wide, bold, technical sans-serif**. Preferred style references include Eurostile/Microgramma-like construction; no commercial font file is bundled and no proprietary typeface is asserted as embedded. Current SVGs use local fallbacks `Eurostile, Microgramma, Arial Black, Arial, sans-serif` and may render differently across devices. For final brand files, outline a properly licensed selected font so logos remain consistent.

Supporting copy: uppercase, increased letter spacing, restrained gray/white.

## Usage rules

1. Do not change the official orange to blue or add the former network rings.
2. Do not stack `Mesh` and `Crossway` in the primary **horizontal** lockup.
3. Keep the symbol silhouette intact; allow breathing room at least equivalent to the height of the smaller inner cutout.
4. Prefer the horizontal lockup for site navigation, README, product headers and documentation. Use symbol-only for icons/favicons where readable.
5. Use the stacked lockup in portrait graphics or centered brand presentations.
6. Do not treat the approved banner as proof of physical enclosure, display firmware, or hardware tests.
7. Keep historic graphics in the repository as design archive, but do not present them as the current logo.
