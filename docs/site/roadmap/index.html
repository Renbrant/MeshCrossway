Development roadmap

From bench prototype to field device.

Display bring-up. Run the native 480×320 UI on the received Waveshare hardware and validate touch, storage and performance.
Radio links. Prove Meshtastic PROTO and MeshCore Companion independently over the selected UARTs.
Dual-network operation. Run both radios concurrently and measure recovery, traffic, RF coexistence and power behavior.
GNSS & field features. Validate MAX-M10S, POIs, tracks, GPX and controlled location sharing.
Carrier PCB & enclosure. Freeze the electrical and mechanical design only after bench gates pass.
Future integrations. Evaluate MeshMapper collaboration, ATAK/CoT and Reticulum where they add real interoperability.
MeshCrossway is an independent open-source experimental project. Meshtastic, MeshCore and referenced hardware brands remain the property of their respective projects and owners. Not a certified emergency device.
