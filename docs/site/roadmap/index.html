Development roadmap

From bench prototype to field device.

Touchscreen and UI. Owner-tested v0.6.8 is the rollback; main contains experimental v0.7.6. Validate proposed v0.7.7 swipe rails, persistent Settings navigation, sender metadata, emoji sizing and AXP2101 battery before release.
Radio links. MeshCore BLE Companion is integrated experimentally. Next: implement Meshtastic protobuf PhoneAPI over its own BLE link and prove two independent concurrent authenticated radio sessions.
Dual-network operation. Run both radios concurrently and measure recovery, traffic, RF coexistence and power behavior.
GNSS & field features. Validate MAX-M10S, POIs, tracks, GPX and controlled location sharing.
Three independent power domains. Validate battery, charge and autonomy per Core and Pod; optional physical docking and any new PCB follow successful BLE and RF tests. Former shared Rev A carrier is archived.
Deferred work. Encrypted message history (eFuse/HMAC + AES-GCM SD journal) is paused pending ChatGPT Work and explicit authorization. Evaluate wanderer, MeshMapper, ATAK/CoT and Reticulum only after core workflows are proven.
