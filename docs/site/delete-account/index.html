Account deletion

Delete your MeshCrossway account.

You can permanently delete your MeshCrossway account and all of its cloud data at any time, from the app or from this website.

What is deleted

  • Your MeshCrossway account (sign-in identity managed by Clerk).
  • All cloud copies of your messages and favorite conversations.
  • All GPX tracks stored in the cloud.

Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone. Our hosting provider’s automatic database backups may keep deleted records for up to 30 days before they expire. Data stored only on your phone (messages, tracks, offline maps and settings) is not affected; remove it in the app or by uninstalling MeshCrossway.

Delete on the web

  1. Open the MeshCrossway account page and sign in with the same account you use in the app.
  2. Scroll to Delete account, type DELETE and select Delete my account.

Go to account deletion →

Delete in the Android app

  1. Open MeshCrossway and go to Account & sync.
  2. Select Delete account and confirm Delete permanently.

Can't sign in anymore? Contact us through the developer contact shown on the Google Play listing and we will delete the account on your behalf. See the privacy policy for details.
