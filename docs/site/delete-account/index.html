Delete your account

You can permanently delete your MeshCrossway account and all of its cloud data at any time, from the app or from this website.

From the Android app

  1. Open MeshCrossway and go to Account & sync.
  2. Tap Delete account and cloud data.
  3. Confirm with Delete permanently.

From the website

  1. Sign in on the account page with the same account you use in the app.
  2. Scroll to Delete account and choose Delete account and cloud data.
  3. Confirm, then type DELETE.

If you can no longer sign in, contact us through the email address on the app's Google Play listing from the email address registered with your account, and we will delete it within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in identity (email address and linked sign-in methods).
  • All synchronized messages and favorite conversations.
  • All GPX tracks stored in the cloud.

Deletion is immediate and permanent; nothing is kept in a recoverable form. Data stored only on your phone (radios, chats, maps and local tracks) is not affected; clear the app's storage or uninstall the app to remove it.
