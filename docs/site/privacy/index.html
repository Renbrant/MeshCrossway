Privacy policy

MeshCrossway privacy policy.

Effective November 16, 2026. This policy covers the MeshCrossway Android app (package com.meshcrossway.mobile), the optional MeshCrossway account and cloud sync, and this website.

The short version

  • MeshCrossway works without an account. Your messages, contacts, tracks, maps and settings stay on your phone unless you turn on cloud sync.
  • There are no ads, no analytics or tracking SDKs, and we never sell or share your data for advertising.
  • If you create an account and enable sync, the data you choose to sync is stored in the cloud under your account. You can delete your account and all cloud data at any time.

Data stored only on your phone

The app stores mesh messages, contacts and nodes heard on each network, favorites, saved points, GPS tracks, downloaded offline map areas and app settings in its private storage on your device. Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer of this data are disabled. This data is not sent to us. It leaves your phone only when you choose to export it (for example a backup ZIP, history JSON or GPX file) or when you enable cloud sync.

Permissions and what they are used for

  • Bluetooth — to find and connect to your own Meshtastic and MeshCore radios. Bluetooth scanning is not used to determine your location.
  • Location (precise) — to show your position on the map, follow your movement and record GPS tracks. Track recording runs as a visible foreground service only after you start it, and stops when you pause or stop it. The app does not request background location access. Whether your radios share a position over the mesh is controlled by your radio settings.
  • Camera — only to scan channel QR codes. Images are processed on the device and are not stored or uploaded.
  • Notifications — to alert you about new messages and show the status of radio connections and track recording.
  • Network and Wi-Fi state — to load maps and, if you turn it on, to run the optional Remote workspace: a local HTTPS server on your phone that other devices on the same Wi-Fi network can open. It is off by default.

Optional account

You can sign in with an account to use cloud sync. Sign-in is provided by Clerk, which processes your email address, name and profile information from the sign-in provider you choose (such as Google or Apple), plus session data needed to keep you signed in.

Optional cloud sync

Cloud sync is off by default and runs only after you sign in, enable it and tap Sync now. It can upload:

  • messages from the last seven days and the history of conversations you mark as favorite, including sender names, conversation identifiers and reception details;
  • your favorite conversations;
  • if you enable track sync separately, up to five saved GPX tracks (5 MiB each). Tracks contain location history.

Cloud sync does not upload Bluetooth radio identifiers, contacts or radio settings. Data is sent over HTTPS and stored on Cloudflare (Workers, D1 and R2). It is not end-to-end encrypted. Ordinary messages are deleted from the cloud after seven days; messages in favorite conversations and tracks are kept until you remove them or delete your account. You can review, download and delete cloud data on the account page.

Other network requests

  • Maps — map tiles and styles are loaded from OpenFreeMap. Like any website, the tile server receives your IP address and the map areas requested.
  • MeshCore presets — when you open MeshCore radio presets, the app may download the public preset list from api.meshcore.nz.
  • Support diagnostic — a report you create and share yourself. It contains app, Android and phone model information and excludes message content, coordinates, names, identifiers, keys and credentials.

Deleting your data

Delete your account and all cloud data from the app (Account & sync → Delete account) or on the web (account deletion). Deletion is immediate; automatic database backups kept by our hosting provider may retain deleted records for up to 30 days before they expire. Data on your phone can be removed in the app or by uninstalling it.

Children

MeshCrossway is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Security

Cloud requests require a valid account session and are limited to the signed-in user's own data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and MeshCrossway is an experimental project.

Changes and contact

We will update this page when data practices change and revise the effective date above. For questions, contact us through the developer contact shown on the Google Play listing, or open an issue on GitHub (do not post personal data in a public issue).
