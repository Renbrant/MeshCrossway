Why MeshCrossway

A crossway between independent mesh ecosystems.

MeshCrossway is an open-source experiment in making off-grid communication simpler: one purpose-built field device, two independent LoRa mesh networks, one coherent human interface.

What it is

At its core, MeshCrossway is a touchscreen host connected to two separate radio computers. One radio participates in Meshtastic and the other in MeshCore. The host does not collapse the protocols into a single network; it presents them through a unified experience while keeping network origin and destination visible.

Who it is for

The design targets hikers, travelers, radio and mesh enthusiasts, preparedness users, field teams and developers exploring resilient communications where internet or cellular coverage may be absent.

What it is not

It is not a certified rescue communicator, satellite messenger or guaranteed-delivery system. It is also not, by default, a Meshtastic↔MeshCore bridge. Cross-network interoperability is a separate technical and policy problem.

MeshCrossway is an independent open-source experimental project. Meshtastic, MeshCore and referenced hardware brands remain the property of their respective projects and owners. Not a certified emergency device.
