## Attribution and technical limitations

These photographs remain the property of their respective rights holders and are **not covered by the MeshCrossway MIT license**. Their inclusion does not authorize unrestricted reuse or imply manufacturer endorsement. Verify actual delivered hardware revision and rely on the matching [Waveshare schematics](https://docs.waveshare.com/ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-3.5/Resources-And-Documents) before PCB assembly.

The retailer's connector numbering and the manufacturer's revision-specific schematic may not match. Identify signals by GPIO labels and verified continuity, not image position. For connections to the **two Seeed XIAO ESP32-S3 + Wio-SX1262 kits**, follow [V1 hardware wiring](../../hardware-wiring.md) and bench-test the proposed external UART.
